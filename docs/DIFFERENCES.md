# Where dbcraft differs from vanilla 26.3

One ranked list of every difference that the documents, the comparators and the code admit to, gathered from the "Not verified", "Not implemented", "Gaps", "differs" and
"known differences" sections of `docs/GAME.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `WORLD.md`, `SURVIVAL.md`, `CONTAINERS.md`, `PLAYER-VISIBILITY.md`, `WORLD-DYNAMICS.md`, `GATEWAY.md`, `NET.md`,
`ENTITY.md`, `WORLD-IMPORT.md`, the `PERF-*.md` and scoreboard documents, `tests/e2e/README.md` ("what the comparisons allow to differ"), the ignore / mask / KEEP lists of every
`tests/e2e/compare_*.py`, and `sql/OVERRIDES.txt`; plus the things found while working on this list (marked "found").  Mobs (class c, `sql/94x`, `510`, `541`, `550`, `551`) are another
track's work and only appear where they touch a line below.

**Classes.**  (a) wrong bytes or behaviour that a player or a client could notice; (b) ordering or timing only (the same packets, in another order or tick); (c) a missing feature;
(d) a difference of the performance model (who does the work, how much, when).  **Impact** is what a player notices (H / M / L); **effort** is S (hours), M (a day), L (days or a
subsystem); **vanilla check** says whether the difference can be proven against the real 26.3 server with the existing harnesses or from the jar's bytecode / registries (Y), only
statistically (S), or not at all because the vanilla value is random per run (N).  **Status** is what this work did about it; "deferred" says exactly what to change.

Ranking: player-visible impact per effort, class (a) first, then (b), then (c), then (d).

## What was fixed in this work

Every fix is proved against recordings of the real vanilla 26.3 server (started with `tests/e2e/vanilla_start.sh`, `-Xms1G -Xmx2G`), by a comparator of `tests/e2e/` and a file of `tests/sql/` that pins recorded values.

1. **Placement states** (item 1; `sql/890_placement.sql`, hooks in `sql/432`): `tests/e2e/place_probe.py` places 509 items against an anchor with chosen face, cursor, yaw, pitch and neighbours on the real server and on dbcraft; `compare_place.py`: **503 of 509 identical** (pillars 42/42, slabs 16/16 including merging, stairs 56/56 including 24 corner shapes, facing blocks 102/102, directional blocks 90/90, trapdoors 24/24, signs, banners and skulls 39/39, torches, lanterns, ladders, levers and buttons 96/96, doors 12/12, fences, panes and bars 26/26, walls 0/6).  Before the work every one of them was placed with the default state.  Found on the way: the placer is told the clicked cell and the cell next to the face (not the target: a merged slab differs), and a use out of reach is answered with the acknowledgement only.  `tests/sql/890_placement.sql`: 322 checks from the recording.
2. **Sounds** (item 2; `sql/838_sounds.sql`; the block sound types are read from the jar by `tools/java/SoundDump.java`, `tools/gen_sounds.sh`, loaded by `tools/load_sounds.py`): `tests/e2e/sound.sh compare`: a placed block (5 block types), hurt, death, the two fall sounds, the seven eating sounds, the eaten item and the burp, the chest lid, `/give`'s pickup sound.  Sounds and the packets that fix their order: `kill` 4/4 identical, `eat` 2/2, `fall` 5/6 (the one forced `block_update` below a landing player), `place` 11/14 (the three left: a random tick of the recorded world and the position of a `set_equipment` against a pickup sound), `chest` 6/8 (open and close are identical, with the observers' arm swings; the other two steps differ by the tick in which the visibility tracker sends a `set_equipment`, item 60).  `tests/sql/838_sounds.sql`: 14 recorded sound packets rebuilt byte for byte (the seed taken from the recording), the range rule (strictly less than 16 blocks, the causer excluded), the block types.  A bug that test found: a SQL function parameter named like a column (`x`) silently compared the column with itself, so everybody heard everything.
3. **Commands** (item 3; `sql/839_cmd_more.sql`): `/experience`, `/damage`, `/kick`, `/defaultgamemode`; `tests/e2e/cmd_mini.py` against the real server (details in `docs/GAME.md`).  The recording taught: a `set ... points` that changes nothing sends no `set_experience`, `query points` is the progress inside the level, the `/damage` amount is a float tag, a plain kick reason is a bare string tag.
4. **Chat and command rate limits** (item 17): `tests/e2e/spam_probe.py`.  Bursts of 9 and 10 chat messages and of 9 and 10 commands are identical; fixed: the counters start at zero in a new session, and the chat threshold test (the 11th message kicks, not the 10th).  Streams at 500 and 200 ms differ by one to two messages (the tick rate of the dev gateway); messages after the offending one in the same tick are still answered (item 61).
5. **Tooling**: `tests/e2e/vanilla_start.sh`, `vanilla_rcon.py`, `dev_cycle.sh`, `reload.sh`, `apply_fn.py`; `compare_survival.py` keeps `update_attributes` (sorted entries); `tools/bootstrap.sh`, `tools/setup-db.sh`, `tests/e2e/setup.sh`, `tests/sql/prun.sh` load the sound data.

Examined and **not fixable**: item 35 (container drag order: the order of a `HashSet<Slot>` by identity hash code).  Examined and **already right**: item 5 (the respawn duplicates), the combat enter / end packets (item 48: vanilla sends them only for damage with an attacker).

## Deferred, in flux: exactly what to change

**Item 30, modifier order inside one attribute.**  `mc.attr_modifiers` / the equipment-to-attribute builder in `sql/902_vis_equipment.sql` (and the memo of `sql/981_attr_memo.sql`)
order the modifiers of an attribute by slot (feet, legs, chest, head, main hand).  Vanilla's `AttributeMap` keeps them in insertion order: the order in which `ServerPlayer` handled
the equipment changes: at join and after a respawn the order in which `LivingEntity.collectEquipmentChanges` walks `EquipmentSlot.VALUES` (ascending ordinal: mainhand, offhand, feet, legs,
chest, head, body, saddle), within one tick; across ticks the order of the changes.  The recorded `pose` steps ("armor", two of them) show helmet and chestplate first, then boots and
leggings: the helmet and chestplate were put on in one `/item replace` before the boots and leggings.  The fix is to remember per player an insertion counter for each equipped slot (a
column next to the slot's item, written when the slot changes) and order the modifiers by that counter instead of by slot.  Proof: `tests/e2e/vis.sh compare`, scenario `pose`, the two
armor steps must become identical.

**Item 31 to 34, 52.**  The pair evaluation (`sql/909`), the `/tp` order and the movement syncs belong to the visibility track; the doc `PLAYER-VISIBILITY.md` "Not verified" section lists
the recorded cases.  The rule that fits two of the three recorded `/tp` cases is "forgets after the bundle for a viewer, removal before the forgets for a target"; no single rule fits
all three, which is why no change is proposed.

## Commands: what remains "not implemented" after this work

Implemented now: `ban clear damage defaultgamemode deop difficulty experience gamemode gamerule give help kick kill list me msg op pardon say seed teleport time weather whitelist` and the aliases.  The cheapest of the rest, by what each needs:
* no new machinery: `/save-all` `/save-on` `/save-off` (messages; the database saves), `/stop` (the gateway's stop flag), `/banlist`, `/setidletimeout`, `/version`, `/perf`, `/stopsound`, `/playsound` (`mc.pkt_sound`);
* the `block_pos` argument (`mc.cmd_parse` has none): `/spawnpoint`, `/setworldspawn`; with `block_state`: `/setblock`, `/fill` (`mc.fill` exists), `/clone`, `/fillbiome`, `/place`;
* the `component` argument (JSON text to NBT): `/title`, `/tellraw`, `/dialog`, `/bossbar`, `/team`;
* a subsystem: `/effect` (effects), `/summon` `/ride` `/spectate` `/tag` (entities), `/scoreboard` `/trigger`, `/worldborder`, `/tick`, `/execute` `/function` `/data` `/item` `/loot` `/attribute` `/advancement` `/recipe` `/schedule` `/random` `/locate` `/spreadplayers` `/waypoint` `/rotate` `/swing`.
